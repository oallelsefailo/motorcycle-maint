ZX-10R (Gen 6) — Torque + Bolt + Tool Reference

Body

Upper fairings
  • M5 × 15mm — 0.8 pitch
  • 8mm socket
  • Torque: ~3–5 ft-lb
Windscreen bolts
  • M5 × 12–15mm — 0.8 pitch
  • 8mm socket
  • Torque: ~3–5 ft-lb
Mirror mounting bolts
  • M6 × 22mm — 1.00 pitch
  • 10mm socket
  • Torque: ~7–9 ft-lb
Front fender
  • M6 × 14–15mm — 1.00 pitch
  • 10mm socket
  • Torque: ~7–9 ft-lb
Bar end weights
  • M8 × 30mm — 1.25 pitch
  • 12mm socket
  • Torque: ~15–20 ft-lb

Brakes

Front brake caliper bolts
  • M10 × 73mm — 1.5 pitch
  • 17mm socket
  • Torque: ~26–30 ft-lb

Driveline

Rear sprocket nuts
  • M10 — 1.25 pitch
  • 17mm socket
  • Torque: ~44 ft-lb

Wheels & Suspension

Rear axle nut
  • M24 × 1.5
  • 32mm socket
  • Torque: 95.9 ft-lb
Front axle nut
  • 22mm or 24mm hex (verify fitment)
  • Torque: ~94–96 ft-lb
Front axle pinch bolts
  • M8 × 35mm — 1.25 pitch
  • 8mm Allen (12mm socket equivalent)
  • Torque: ~15–20 ft-lb
  • Note: tighten alternately
Front brake caliper bolts (shared for quick access)
  • M10 × 73mm — 1.5 pitch
  • 17mm socket
  • Torque: ~26–30 ft-lb
Upper triple crown pinch bolts
  • M8 × 25mm — 1.25 pitch
  • 12mm socket
  • Torque: ~15–20 ft-lb
Lower triple clamp pinch bolts
  • M8 × 40mm — 1.25 pitch
  • 12mm socket
  • Torque: ~15–20 ft-lb
Öhlins steering damper mounts
  • M8 × 25mm — 1.25 pitch
  • 12mm socket
  • Torque: 97 lb-in (~8 ft-lb)

Quick Reference

  • M5 → 8mm → ~3–5 ft-lb
  • M6 → 10mm → ~7–9 ft-lb
  • M8 → 12mm / 8mm Allen → ~15–20 ft-lb
  • M10 → 17mm → ~26–44 ft-lb
  • Front Axle → 22–24mm hex → ~94–96 ft-lb
  • Rear Axle → 32mm → 95.9 ft-lb
